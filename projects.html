Independent publication · Current focus

Higher Ideals

Essays on democracy, public policy, civic engagement, and public service.

Higher Ideals explores American history, democratic institutions, public policy, and political life through research-driven essays and historical investigations.

It asks difficult questions about public life, examines competing arguments fairly, and applies consistent standards across ideological lines. The aim is to encourage informed citizenship and thoughtful debate—not simply to add more noise to the conversation.
